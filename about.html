Who We Are

Universal Recruiting Company

For over 25 years, connecting businesses with the industry's best talent — nationwide and worldwide.

For over 25 years, we've been helping businesses identify, qualify, and onboard top-tier professionals across a wide range of industries. Our extensive experience and deep network of talent spans:

  • › Labor & Workforce Management (WFM)
  • › Human Capital Management (HCM)
  • › Benefits Administration & Compliance
  • › HR & Payroll
  • › PEO & BPO
  • › Financial Technology (FinTech)
  • › Financial Services
  • › Commercial HVAC & Air Supply
  • › Enterprise Project Management (EPM)
  • › OpenAI (Development & Sales)
  • › C-Level Executives
  • › Executive Assistants

We specialize in identifying and attracting the industry's best — sales executives, sales engineers, implementation specialists, software engineers, and architects — most of whom aren't actively seeking new opportunities. We partner closely with our clients to understand their staffing needs, challenges, goals, and culture, then deliver customized solutions that align with their expectations and budget.

Our team of dedicated recruiters shares a common passion for excellence, and our mission is straightforward: to provide exceptional service and value to both clients and candidates, foster trust, and build lasting partnerships.
