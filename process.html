Our Search Process

A disciplined, six-step approach that keeps the search moving and the quality high.

Step by Step

A Search Done Right

A Day
in the
Search.
Step 1
Discovery Call — We listen to your exact requirements, culture, and ideal candidate profile.
Step 2
Targeted Search — We activate our national and global candidate network specific to your industry.
Step 3
Curated Submissions — We present a qualified slate with resumes and detailed candidate profiles.
Step 4
Interview Coordination — We handle all scheduling, logistics, and travel arrangements.
Step 5
Feedback & Refinement — We gather your feedback and refine the search until we find the right fit.
Step 6
Offer & Placement — We consult on negotiation, finalize the placement, and conduct reference checks.
